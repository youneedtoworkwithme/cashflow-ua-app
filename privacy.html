Privacy Policy (English)

Cashflow UA stores local single-device game progress strictly on your device. No personal data, advertising IDs, or behavioral tracking are collected or sold. Optional multiplayer rooms transmit only the chosen display nickname and game board state required for turn synchronization. Purchases are processed securely by Apple App Store / Google Play.
